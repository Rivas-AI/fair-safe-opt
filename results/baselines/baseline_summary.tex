% ---------- Notes ----------
\section*{Notes}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Training: ERM, up to 10 epochs, early stopping patience = 5, lr = $10^{-4}$.
  \item Small MLP: hidden dim = 64, dropout = 0.0.
  \item Data splits seeded per run (\texttt{random\_state = seed}).
  \item DPD = Demographic Parity Difference; EOD = Equal Opportunity Difference; TPR = True Positive Rate.
  \item The logistic model shows a large TPR gap between men (0.53) and women (0.30), indicating the model classifies high-income women at a much lower rate despite similar overall accuracy.
  \item The small MLP closes the TPR gap substantially (0.65 vs.\ 0.56) while also improving overall accuracy and AUC.
\end{itemize}
